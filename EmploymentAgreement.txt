EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement establishes the terms between the employer and employee.

The employee shall maintain confidentiality of confidential information obtained during employment. Intellectual property created within the scope of employment shall belong to the employer.

Either party may terminate the agreement by providing written notice. The employee shall comply with applicable company policies and governing law.

Any dispute concerning this agreement may be submitted to arbitration. The parties acknowledge their obligations concerning liability, indemnity, and protection of confidential information.
